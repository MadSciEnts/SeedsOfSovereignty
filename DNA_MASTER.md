# SEED OF SOVEREIGNTY: THE MASTER DNA (v2.2.0)

## 1. CORE MATHEMATICS & SPATIAL LOGIC
### 1.1 Spatial Scaling (GSF 0.1)
- **Standard:** 128x72 baseline. 1.0u = 100px.

## 2. THE 4-EPOCH EVOLUTION
### 2.1 Epoch 1: The Pilot (T1-12)
- **Propulsion:** Direct 'Impulse' Control. Ships are highly maneuverable.
- **Interface:** Primary Left Surface utilizes a [Joystick] for direct orientation and thrust.

### 2.2 Epoch 2: The Commander (T13-24)
- **Propulsion:** Dual-Mode [Manual Impulse] or [Tactical Nav]. 
- **Interface:** Primary Left Surface is switchable. Larger ships (Carriers/Battleships) prioritize waypoint-based navigation via [Tactical Touchpad].

## 3. SHIP TIER & MODULE ARCHITECTURE (v4.0.0)
### 3.1 Ship Classifications
- **T1-T3:** [Primary x1, Secondary x1]

### 3.2 Command Logic Invariants
- **Primary Weapons:** Controlled in concert via a singular [Attack Joystick] (Right Side).
- **Engine Control:** Locked to the **Bottom-Left screen anchor**. 
    - Mode A (Impulse): [Joystick]
    - Mode B (Tactical Nav): [Tactical Touchpad]
- **Command Matrix (Right Side):** Handles Payload (Drones/Fighters) and Systems (Core/Shields).
