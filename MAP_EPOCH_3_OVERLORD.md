\# MAP: EPOCH III (Tiers 25–36) - The Overlord



\## 1. Core Loop

\- \*\*Objective:\*\* Defend and expand a Central Space Station. 

\- \*\*Gameplay:\*\* Perspective becomes fixed (or slow-rotation). Focus shifts to resource logistics, structural modularity, and "Wave Defense."



\## 2. Key Mechanics

\- \*\*Modular Expansion:\*\* Attaching "Pods" (Laboratories, Shipyards, Turret Arrays) to the station core.

\- \*\*The Drone Hive:\*\* Instead of direct flight, you deploy autonomous wings of T1–T12 ships (your old "DNA") to intercept threats.

\- \*\*Resource Siphoning:\*\* Deploying harvesters to nearby moons or asteroid belts to feed the station's growth.



\## 3. Technical Specs

\- \*\*Scale:\*\* The station is 10x larger than a Tier 24 Flagship. 

\- \*\*Systems:\*\* Deep energy management. You must route power between "Shields," "Industry," and "Research."

\- \*\*Visuals:\*\* Introduction of "Mega-Structure" shaders (internal lights, massive docking arms).
