# DNA: WEAPON LOGIC & PROJECTILE GENESIS

## Rule 1: Tier Gating
- A ship may only find, purchase, and equip modules that match its current Evolution Tier. A Tier 1 ship cannot use a Tier 2 module.

## Rule 2: Power Progression (The "No-Op" Rule)
- A rare, high-damage weapon of a given tier must not have a higher base damage output than a common weapon of the *next* tier. This ensures that evolution is always a meaningful power increase.

## Rule 3: The Effect System (Multi-Faceted Impacts)
- Projectiles carry a `List<ProjectileEffect>`.
- An effect can target any ship attribute (`HULL_DAMAGE`, `SHIELD_DAMAGE`, `SPEED_MULTIPLIER`, `COOLDOWN_MULTIPLIER`).
- Effects can be instant (damage) or have a duration (lingering acid).

## Rule 4: Visual Language (Color & Shape)
- A projectile's color and shape are directly linked to its primary effect.
- **Kinetic (Yellow/Orange):** Primary Hull/Shield damage.
- **Energy (Violet/Purple):** High shield damage, low hull damage.
- **Pulse (Gold/Cyan):** System disruption (slows, cooldown increase).
- **Exotic (Green/Red):** Lingering effects (acid) or shield-piercing.

## Rule 5: Impact VFX Granularity
- The `vfx` property on a `ProjectileEffect` determines its impact visual.
- A single projectile can trigger multiple VFX (e.g., a `SHIELD_RIPPLE_BLUE` on shield contact and a `SPARK_RED` on hull contact).
- Lingering effects must spawn continuous, low-intensity particle effects for their duration.

## Rule 6: Strategic Specialization (The "All-or-Nothing" Rule)
- Modules can be hyper-specialized. A module may have effects that *only* target shields, or it may have a `SHIELD_PIERCE` effect and no shield damage at all, applying all its potency directly to the hull.

## Rule 7: Rarity & Deviation
- All modules have a `Rarity` (`COMMON` to `LEGENDARY`).
- `COMMON` modules are the baseline for a tier.
- Higher rarity modules are generated by deviating from this baseline: adding more effects, increasing potencies, or introducing strategic downsides (e.g., high power, low speed).
