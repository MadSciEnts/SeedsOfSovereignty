\# DNA: VISUAL PALETTES \& SHADERS



\## 1. The "High-Bit" Palette Logic

\- \*\*Primary:\*\* High-saturation (The "Energy" color).

\- \*\*Secondary:\*\* Mid-tone (The "Hull" color).

\- \*\*Tertiary:\*\* Dark/Desaturated (The "Shadow/Detail" color).

\- \*\*Accent:\*\* Neon/White (The "Specular/Warning" highlights).



\## 2. Faction Palettes

\- \*\*The Vanguard (Player):\*\* Deep Blues, Slate Grey, Cyan Exhaust.

\- \*\*The Swarm (Enemy):\*\* Toxic Greens, Obsidian, Purple Core.

\- \*\*The Ancients (Bosses):\*\* Gold, White, Crimson Energy.



\## 3. Shader Standards

\- \*\*Bloom:\*\* Every "Energy" pixel ($Value > 0.8$) must bleed light into neighboring pixels.

\- \*\*Chromatic Aberration:\*\* Apply a slight 1-pixel RGB shift during "Hull Breach" or "Warp" events.

\- \*\*Scanlines:\*\* A subtle 10% opacity overlay to give a "Terminal" feel to the HUD.
