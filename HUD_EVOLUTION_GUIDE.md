\# HUD EVOLUTION: Epoch Transitions



\## 1. Epoch I (The Pilot)

\- \*\*Focus:\*\* Health, Shield, Energy, and Minimap.

\- \*\*Style:\*\* "Cockpit View." Gauges are curved around the edges of the screen.



\## 2. Epoch II (The Admiral)

\- \*\*Focus:\*\* Fleet Cohesion, Squad Status, Target Command.

\- \*\*Style:\*\* "Tactical Overlay." Gauges become linear bars to make room for the fleet command menu.



\## 3. Epoch III (The Overlord)

\- \*\*Focus:\*\* Power Distribution, Structural Integrity, Harvest Rates.

\- \*\*Style:\*\* "Blueprint View." The HUD becomes a schematic of the Space Station itself.



\## 4. Epoch IV (The Sovereign)

\- \*\*Focus:\*\* Sector Influence, Resource Totals, Threat Levels.

\- \*\*Style:\*\* "The Nexus." Minimalist data-overlay across the entire screen. Most info is hidden until a node is tapped.
