\# MAP: EPOCH II (Tiers 13–24) - The Admiral



\## 1. Core Loop

\- \*\*Objective:\*\* Build and maintain an Armada. The player ship is now a "Flagship."

\- \*\*Combat:\*\* Squad-based tactics. You assign "DNA Templates" to your escort ships.



\## 2. New Mechanics

\- \*\*Formations:\*\* Toggle between Wedge, Sphere, and Echelon.

\- \*\*Fleet Sync:\*\* Command subordinates to focus fire on a single massive target.

\- \*\*Logistics:\*\* Managing the "Fleet Cohesion" bar. If it drops, AI escorts become erratic.



\## 3. Technical Specs for Epoch II

\- \*\*Perspective:\*\* Camera zooms out to show the entire squadron.

\- \*\*Unit Logic:\*\* Sub-ships use Fleks ECS AI systems to maintain formation while dodging.

\- \*\*Input:\*\* Shift to "Tap-to-Target" and "Formation Select" UI.
