\# SYSTEMS: DYNAMIC GALAXY EVENTS



\## 1. Faction Tension

\- \*\*Logic:\*\* Each sector has a Tension value ($0.0$ to $1.0$).

\- \*\*Warfare:\*\* When Tension > 0.8, massive fleet battles (Epoch II style) trigger autonomously in the background.



\## 2. The Nemesis Protocol

\- \*\*Logic:\*\* If a specific procedural enemy kills the player, that enemy's ID is saved.

\- \*\*Evolution:\*\* The next time that enemy appears, it has +1 Tier and a unique "Slayer" visual glow.



\## 3. Sovereign Shifts

\- \*\*Logic:\*\* High-tier events that affect low-tier players (e.g., a Star collapsing in Epoch IV creates a black hole hazard for a Tier 5 Pilot in that sector).
