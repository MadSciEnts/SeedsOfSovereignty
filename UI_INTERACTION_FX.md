\# UI INTERACTION: JUICE \& HAPTICS



\## 1. Visual Feedback

\- \*\*Tap Ripple:\*\* A subtle, high-speed procedural circle expands from the touch point ($t < 0.2s$).

\- \*\*Data Ghosting:\*\* When text changes (like "Shields: 80%"), the old numbers should "flicker" and fade upward before the new numbers appear.



\## 2. Haptic Rules (Android)

\- \*\*Soft Pulse:\*\* Triggered on menu navigation.

\- \*\*Heavy Jolt:\*\* Triggered on ship hull damage or "Sovereign Event."

\- \*\*Constant Thrum:\*\* Low-intensity vibration during "Warp" or "Hyper-Jump" transitions.



\## 3. Audio Sync

\- \*\*The "Blip":\*\* Every menu tap triggers a short, high-pitched procedural beep ($1000Hz$ to $2000Hz$ sweep).

\- \*\*The "Hum":\*\* Background UI has a low-frequency $60Hz$ brownian noise to simulate power running through a terminal.
