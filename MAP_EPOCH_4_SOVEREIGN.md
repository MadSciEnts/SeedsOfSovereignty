\# MAP: EPOCH IV (Tiers 37–48) - The Sovereign



\## 1. Core Loop

\- \*\*Objective:\*\* Total Galactic Synchronization.

\- \*\*Gameplay:\*\* Grand Strategy. You no longer control one entity; you control the "Will" of a network of stations and fleets across a Sector Map.



\## 2. Key Mechanics

\- \*\*The Nexus:\*\* Instant communication between all owned assets.

\- \*\*Star-Folding:\*\* The ability to warp reality (e.g., creating wormholes, collapsing stars for energy).

\- \*\*The Great Filter:\*\* Massive, galaxy-wide threats (Void Entities) that require the coordination of every asset you've built since Tier 1.



\## 3. Technical Specs

\- \*\*UI:\*\* Shift to a "Strategic Overlay" (Node-based map).

\- \*\*Performance:\*\* Fleet battles are now calculated via "Mass-Simulation" systems rather than individual ship AI to maintain Android performance.

\- \*\*The Win State:\*\* Reaching Tier 48 and achieving "Galactic Singularity."
