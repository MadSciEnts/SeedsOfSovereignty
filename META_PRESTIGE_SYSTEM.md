\# META: PRESTIGE \& ANCIENT TECH



\## 1. The Persistence Logic

\- \*\*DNA Essence:\*\* Earned by defeating higher-tier enemies or completing Epoch Zeniths.

\- \*\*Carry-over:\*\* When a player dies or "Ascends," Essence is used to buy permanent upgrades (Ancient Tech).



\## 2. Ancient Tech Tree

\- \*\*Memory Lattice:\*\* Increases the starting Tier of a new run (e.g., Start at T3 instead of T1).

\- \*\*Hardpoint Echoes:\*\* Adds a permanent +5% damage to all procedural weapons.

\- \*\*Void Shielding:\*\* A small chance to ignore fatal damage once per mission.



\## 3. Storage Protocol (Android)

\- \*\*JSON Serialization:\*\* Use `ktx-serialization` or LibGDX `Json` to store the meta-state in `Gdx.files.local("meta.json")`.

\- \*\*Encryption:\*\* Use a simple XOR obfuscation to prevent casual save-editing on Android.
