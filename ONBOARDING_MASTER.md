# THE SEEDS OF SOVEREIGNTY: Project Onboarding

## 1. Project Vision
A 2D top-down space evolution saga for Android. 
- **Evolution:** 48 Tiers of power across 4 Epochs (Pilot, Admiral, Overlord, Sovereign).
- **Style:** Procedural High-Bit Pixel Art. 
- **Mechanics:** "Swap or Scrap" gameplay (No Inventory). High-fidelity procedural VFX and UI.

## 2. Technical Stack (2026 Standard)
- **Engine:** LibGDX (v1.12+) with LibKTX (Kotlin Extensions).
- **Logic:** Fleks ECS (Entity Component System).
- **UI:** LibKTX `scene2d` DSL + ShapeDrawer for procedural borders.
- **Rendering:** SpriteBatch for entities, custom Shaders for Nebulae and VFX.
- **Persistence:** JSON-based state saving for Tiers and "Ancient Tech" perks.
